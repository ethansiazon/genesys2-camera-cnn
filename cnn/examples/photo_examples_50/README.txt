50 MODEL PHOTO EXAMPLES

20 examples from the registered TRAIN dataset; 15 successful and 15 failed VALIDATION frames from the retained V5 epoch8 fixed-point CNN + integer decoder + frozen R3 tracking replay. Green is labelled drone; magenta is model output; blue is labelled other aircraft. Success means active output and IoU >=0.5. These deliberately selected examples DO NOT estimate overall accuracy. No new inference or training was run. TRACKING/MOTION/PREPROCESSING are still CPU software; assumed90ms latency is not actual board speed. TRAIN photos show registered source pixels, not exact random augmented tensors; individual SGD draw membership was not reconstructed.

Open index.html for a gallery, or browse the three numbered image folders. Every photo has its own label and legend. Magnified labelled crops show tiny targets more clearly. A wrong box can be near the drone yet have incorrect size. Original files and model are unchanged.
